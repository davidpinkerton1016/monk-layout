# White Mage keyboard layout

Level 100 PvE preset. Open `/whmlayout` and choose **Apply White Mage layout**. This preset uses HUD 3, White Mage hotbars 1-3, the compact spacing from the Monk preset, a centered lily gauge and a party list near the center-left of the screen.

| Key | Normal | Shift | Ctrl |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Glare III | Glare IV | Medica III |
| 2 | Dia | Afflatus Misery | Cure III |
| 3 | Holy III | Presence of Mind | Plenary Indulgence |
| 4 | Afflatus Solace | Tetragrammaton | Temperance |
| 5 | Afflatus Rapture | Cure II | Divine Caress |
| R | Assize | Asylum | Liturgy of the Bell |
| F | Regen | Divine Benison | Rescue |
| G | Swiftcast | Thin Air | Limit Break |
| Z | Benediction | Aquaveil | Cure, click only |
| X | Aetherial Shift | Surecast | Medica, click only |
| C | Esuna | Raise | Repose, click only |
| V | Sprint | Lucid Dreaming | Empty |

The last four Ctrl slots do not receive keyboard bindings. Cure, Medica and Repose occupy three visible slots for clicking. Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V retain their existing functions. Both presets assign the same 32 global hotbar shortcuts, including X for movement, Shift+X for knockback protection and V for Sprint.

Choose a party member in the party list before using a targeted heal. Swiftcast is on G and Raise on Shift+C. Asylum and Liturgy of the Bell use the game's ground-target placement. The preset keeps all 35 assignable White Mage, healer-role and common combat actions available, including the lower-level heals for synced content.

The stored base actions are Stone, Aero, Holy and Medica II. FFXIV's traits display Glare III, Dia, Holy III and Medica III at level 100. Glare IV and Divine Caress also have dedicated slots, so they remain accessible with either action-change preference.

White Mage has its own first backup and restore index. Existing Monk backups remain readable. Applying White Mage edits HUD 3 and White Mage's saved hotbars; Monk's HUD 4 and saved hotbar contents remain available. Keyboard bindings and hotbar sharing options are character-wide settings. The plugin does not automatically switch HUD slots when changing jobs: select HUD 3 for White Mage or HUD 4 for Monk through the game's HUD layout selector.

Action names and upgrades were checked against the [official White Mage job guide](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/jobguide/whitemage/). Key positions are an ergonomic starting preset, with frequently used attacks and lily heals on unmodified keys.
